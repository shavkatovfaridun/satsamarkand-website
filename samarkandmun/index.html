About the Conference

Diplomacy Begins Here

Cambridge MUN 2026 is Samarkand's first large-scale Model United Nations conference — bringing together 100 high school delegates across five committees to debate real global issues through formal diplomacy and negotiation.

Series I marks the inaugural edition of MUN Samarkand, launched by SAT Samarkand in partnership with Cambridge Unit School. It's designed to give Samarkand students their first real access to competitive, high-level Model UN — without traveling to Tashkent.

Every delegate receives an official certificate of participation, with awards recognizing outstanding performance in each committee.

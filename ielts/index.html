How We Train

Every skill. Every week.

We don't skip skills or rotate them monthly. All four are practised every single week so nothing atrophies before exam day.

Reading

Skimming, scanning, matching headings, True/False/Not Given. Speed and accuracy drills on academic passages.

40 questions · 60 min

Writing

Task 1 (graphs, charts, maps) and Task 2 (essays). Every task is marked by the instructor with band-score feedback.

2 tasks · 60 min

Listening

Four sections with varied accents (UK, AU, US). Note completion, multiple choice, and matching question types — all practised weekly.

40 questions · 30 min

Speaking

Part 1 (interview), Part 2 (cue card long turn), Part 3 (discussion). Live practice with instructor feedback on fluency, lexis, and coherence.

3 parts · 11–14 min

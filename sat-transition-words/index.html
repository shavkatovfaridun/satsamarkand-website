Read Before You Guess

Watch-Out Words

These words swing between categories depending on the sentence — memorizing a single meaning is exactly how students miss them on test day.

● 5 flagged words
DUAL-USE

Same Word, Different Job

While
Sequence (“during this time”) in Continue vs. contrast (“whereas”) in Contradict — the single most mistested transition on the SAT. Read the whole sentence before choosing a lane.
Since
Cause (“because”) vs. time (“since then”). The SAT tests both senses — don’t auto-assume causation.
So
On the SAT, “so” is always the causal connector (“therefore”), never the casual intensifier (“so good”).
Still
Almost always a contrast word on the SAT (“nevertheless”), not a literal continuation in time.
Ultimately
Sequence/finality (“in the end”) in Continue, but sometimes summarizes a consequence, which reads more like Cause-and-Effect. Judge by context, not by memory.
